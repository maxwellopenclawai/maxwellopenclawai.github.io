Jarvis

A private personal assistant. Not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

Jarvis is used by one person, its owner, on their own computer.

What Jarvis accesses

The owner's Google Calendar events and list of calendars, only to show their schedule and to add, move or remove events when the owner asks.

How the data is used

Only to manage the owner's own calendar. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising. No other Google data (Gmail, Drive, contacts) is accessed.

Where it is stored

On the owner's own computer. Sign-in details are kept in an encrypted credential store and are never written to notes or logs.

Removing access

Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access → Jarvis → Delete all connections.
